# Calendar event dates (`assets/events.json`)

The calendar follows the South Asian (Khoja / H. Karmali, Mumbai) tradition,
so it marks some days that Arab and Iranian calendars do not, and the other
way round. Where the classical sources disagree, more than one date can be
right. A date with **no source behind it** is not one of those, so it is
corrected. This page records the dates that were checked, so a later edit
does not put an old one back.

Sources: Shaykh al-Mufid (*al-Irshad*, *Masar al-Shi'a*, *al-Jamal*),
Shaykh al-Tusi (*Misbah al-Mutahajjid*, *al-Ghayba*), al-Kulayni, al-Kaf'ami,
Shaykh Abbas Qummi, read through WikiShia (en/fa) and al-islam.org.

## Corrected (2026-10)

| Was | Now | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 16 Muharram: change of qibla | 15 Rajab | The qibla changed 16-17 months after the Hijrah (Rabi' I, 1 AH), which is Rajab 2 AH. 16 Muharram 2 AH would be only 10 months. |
| 19 Safar: Abraha attacked the Kaaba | removed | 17 Muharram already had the same event. The sources put the elephant in Muharram. |
| 10 Shawwal: start of Ghaibat al-Kubra | 15 Sha'ban (329 AH) | The Major Occultation began with the death of the 4th deputy, Ali ibn Muhammad al-Samari, in mid-Sha'ban 329. No source gives 10 Shawwal. |
| 17 Shawwal: Battle of Uhud | 15 Shawwal (3 AH), with the martyrdom of Hazrat Hamza | 15 Shawwal is the Shia date, and 7 Shawwal is the other common date. No source gives the 17th. |
| 17 Jumada I: Battle of Jamal | 10 Jumada I (36 AH) | The date al-Mufid gives in *al-Jamal* (al-Tabari gives mid-Jumada II). No source gives the 17th. |
| 17 Rabi' I: Imam al-Sadiq born 82 AH | 83 AH | al-Kulayni and al-Mufid. 80 AH is the minority report. |
| 15 Ramazan: Imam Hasan born 2 AH | 3 AH | al-Mufid. It also fits Imam Husain's birth in Sha'ban 4 AH. |
| 1 Safar: Siffin "36-37 AH" | 37 AH | The fighting began in Safar 37. |

## Days marked on more than one date (left as they are)

These follow real, differing reports. The app keeps the days the community
observes:

- **Imam Hasan's martyrdom**: 28 Safar (al-Mufid, al-Tusi; the app's date).
  7 Safar comes from al-Shahid al-Awwal, and many present-day maraji' (Sistani,
  Wahid Khorasani, Safi Golpaygani) observe it. The app marks 7 Safar as the
  birth of Imam Musa al-Kazim. That report is weaker: the more common date is
  Dhul Hijjah 127/128 at al-Abwa.
- **Imam al-Rida's martyrdom**: 17 Safar, the end of Safar (29/30), and
  23 Dhul Qa'dah.
- **Imam al-Hadi's birth**: 15 Dhul Hijjah 212 (the best-known date), and
  2 and 5 Rajab. The app's year 214 is the minority report.
- **Lady Fatimah's martyrdom**: 13 Jumada I (75 days) and 3 Jumada II
  (95 days).
- **Lady Zainab's birth**: 5 Jumada I and 1 Sha'ban. **Imam Zayn al-Abidin's
  birth**: 15 Jumada I and 5 Sha'ban.
- **Lady Masuma's death**: 10 (or 12) Rabi' II, and 4 Rabi' I in
  South Asian calendars.
- **Hazrat Abu Talib's death**: 26 Rajab (al-Tusi) and Ramazan (al-Ya'qubi,
  three days after Lady Khadija). 25 Rabi' I has no source found yet.

## Unverified, to check against the printed calendar

10 Safar (Sakina; Arab and Iranian calendars give the child who died in
Damascus as Ruqayya, and the historical Sakina died in 117 AH), 20 Rajab
(Sakina's birth), 10 Jumada II (Mu'tah: the sources say Jumada I, 8 AH),
12 Rajab (Abbas ibn Abd al-Muttalib: WikiShia gives 14 Rajab 32 AH),
21 Jumada II (Umm Kulthum), 5 Dhul Hijjah (Abu Dharr), 23 Dhul Hijjah
(the sons of Muslim), 7 Sha'ban (Qasim), 11 Sha'ban 44 AH (Ali Akbar:
WikiShia gives 33 AH), 29 Rajab (Tabuk) and 25 Rabi' I (Abu Talib).
