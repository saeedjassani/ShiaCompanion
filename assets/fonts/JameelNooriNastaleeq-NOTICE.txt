Jameel Noori Nastaleeq
======================

File:
  assets/fonts/jameel-noori-nastaleeq.ttf   "Jameel Noori Nastaleeq" Version 1; 2008

Source: the "Jameel Noori Nastaleeq.ttf" released by Urdu Jahan
(urdujahan.com, now offline), as mirrored for Arch Linux at
https://github.com/abid-mujtaba/ttf-jameel-noori-nastaleeq (tag v1.1.0).

Licence

This app is free and uses it for Urdu text only.

Changes
-------
Cut down with fontTools' pyftsubset to the Arabic-script letters (with every
ligature and positioning rule that uses them), space and the joiner and
direction controls, and its hinting dropped:

  pyftsubset "Jameel Noori Nastaleeq.ttf" \
    --unicodes="U+0020,U+00A0,U+0600-06FF,U+0750-077F,U+08A0-08FF,U+200C-200F,U+FB50-FDFF,U+FE70-FEFF" \
    --layout-features='*' --no-hinting \
    --output-file=jameel-noori-nastaleeq.ttf

Leaving out its Latin letters and 0-9 is deliberate: English words in Urdu
text then come from the system font (see lib/theme/app_theme.dart).
