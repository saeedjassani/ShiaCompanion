QuranWBW IndoPak Quran text and font
====================================

Files:
  assets/quran/quranwbw-indopak.json   IndoPak Quran text, Madinah edition
  assets/fonts/quranwbw-indopak.ttf    "AlQuran Neo 5x2 Arabic" v2.100

Source: https://quranwbw.com
  text: https://static.quranwbw.com/data/v4/words-data/arabic/3.json?version=7
        ("Qalam Digital Font (Madinah Edition)" on quranwbw.com)
  font: https://quranwbw.com/fonts/AlQuranNeo5x2.woff2

Used with the written permission of QuranWBW.com (granted September 2026).
The font's own licence text reads:

  NOT FOR SALE, NOT FOR MODIFICATION, NOT FOR DISTRIBUTION OR NOT FOR
  DEVELOPMENT WITHOUT WRITTEN NOTICE BY QURANWBW.COM

Font credits, from the font's name table: made by Ayman Siddiqui, based on
the Al Qalam Quran Majeed fonts; ayah numbers from the KFGQPC Nastaleeq font.
QuranWBW credits the IndoPak text to typemybook.com, originally by InPage.

Both files are bundled verbatim. The JSON is the file as served. The font was
converted from WOFF2 to TTF, because Flutter cannot load WOFF2; this is a
container change only, and every glyph and the character map are identical.

The text uses private-use codepoints for pause marks, rukuʿ signs and ayah
numbers. It must be rendered in this font. Qalam uses private-use codepoints
of its own, and they mean different things.
