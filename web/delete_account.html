Account Deletion

You can delete your Shia Companion account and synced favorites through the web app or directly inside the app settings.

Open Deletion Page View Privacy Policy

Fastest Option

1. Open the web deletion page

Visit https://shia-companion.web.app/delete-account.

2. Sign in

If you are not already signed in, use the sign-in button on that page.

3. Confirm deletion

Use the Delete My Account action to permanently remove the account and synced favorites.

In-App Option

  1. Open Shia Companion.
  2. Go to Preferences.
  3. Sign in if needed.
  4. Tap Delete My Account.
  5. Confirm the deletion prompt.

What Gets Deleted

Deleted

  • Your Shia Companion account sign-in record.
  • Your synced favorites tied to that account.

May be retained

  • Anonymous or aggregated analytics data.
  • Crash diagnostics that can no longer be linked back to your account.

Need Help?

If you cannot access the account you want deleted, email developer110@hotmail.com and include the email address associated with the account.
